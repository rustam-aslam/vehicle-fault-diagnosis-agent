Engine Fault Diagnosis

Fault: Engine overheating

Possible causes:
- Low coolant
- Cooling fan failure
- Thermostat failure
- Radiator blockage

Recommended checks:
- Check coolant level
- Check cooling fan operation
- Inspect thermostat
- Inspect radiator


Fault: Engine misfire

Possible causes:
- Faulty spark plug
- Ignition coil failure
- Fuel injector problem
- Air-fuel mixture issue

Recommended checks:
- Inspect spark plugs
- Check ignition coils
- Check injector operation
- Review engine sensor data
