Transmission Fault Diagnosis

Fault: Transmission slipping during acceleration

Possible causes:
- Low transmission fluid
- Worn clutch components
- Hydraulic pressure problems
- Faulty transmission solenoid

Recommended checks:
- Check transmission fluid level
- Check transmission fluid condition
- Monitor hydraulic pressure
- Check transmission solenoid operation


Fault: Harsh shifting

Possible causes:
- Low transmission fluid
- Faulty shift solenoid
- Incorrect transmission calibration

Recommended checks:
- Check transmission fluid level
- Read transmission diagnostic codes
- Check shift solenoid operation
- Verify calibration parameters
